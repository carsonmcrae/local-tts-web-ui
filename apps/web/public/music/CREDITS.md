# Background music: credits and licences

TTSNative's background music is a small library of recordings that their creators placed in the
public domain (CC0 1.0, or a public-domain release). Anyone may copy, change, share and sell them,
also inside other work, without asking and without giving credit. We credit every creator anyway.

That covers shipping them in TTSNative's public source repository, in its installers and in
audio people make with it, with or without changes. Each file contains only the audio: no cover
art, titles or other tags from the originals.

Each track here is an edited excerpt: trimmed, blended at the end so it loops without a seam, matched
in loudness to sit below the narration, cut into chunks of about 20 seconds that each carry 20 ms of
their neighbours (the player crossfades every join over those), and encoded as Opus in WebM, one
folder per track. How each was made, and the
SHA-256 of the original download, is recorded in `apps/web/src/features/music/library.json` in the
source repository, and `npm run music:build` rebuilds the files from the originals.

Licences were checked on each source page on the date shown. If you believe a track is listed
wrongly, please open an issue at https://github.com/carsonmcrae/ttsnative/issues.

## Notes

- Musopen, which published the four piano recordings (the Gymnopédie, the Nocturne, and the two
  Beethoven movements), asks as a courtesy that its recordings are credited and not sold on their own.
  TTSNative is free and only plays them under narration.
- The five nature recordings are Freesound's public high-quality previews of the CC0 originals.
  “Gentle ocean waves” is itself a mix that includes parts of two other CC0 recordings, by subtyrant
  and indieground; both were checked on their own pages (listed under that track).
- Public-domain music can still be matched by mistake by automated copyright systems on video
  sites. The source pages above are the evidence that these recordings are free to use.
