# Legal and privacy review: implementation plan

Companion to [legal-risk-audit.md](legal-risk-audit.md) (snapshot of 20 October 2026). It lists the
straightforward technical and documentation changes from the review, what was done, and what waits for
the maintainer's decision. Nothing here changes the MIT licence, removes a feature or adds a step for
users.
