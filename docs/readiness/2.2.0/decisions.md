# Decision register: 2.2.0 consolidation

Every open question from the consolidated session report, resolved. **Implement** means it is part of
the consolidation branch; **Done** means verified on `main` or the branch; **Defer** keeps the work and
its research for a later release; **Owner** needs the maintainer because it involves credentials,
publishing, money, legal questions or deleting personal data; **Abandon** means the evidence doesn't
justify it.
