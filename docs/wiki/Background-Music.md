# Background music and the audio mixer

TTSNative can play quiet music under the narration: ambient pads, a fantasy theme, lo-fi, piano
pieces, or the sound of rain, a stream or the sea. It is off unless you turn it on, it has its own volume, and it never
changes the narration.

## The audio mixer

The **Audio mixer** button (three sliders) in the player at the bottom opens every sound level in one
place. On a phone it is next to the play controls; on a wider screen it is at the end of the position
bar.

- **Narration**: how loud the voice is, from 0 to 100 %. It starts at 100 % and is remembered on this
  device. On iPhone and iPad the narration follows the device's own volume buttons instead, and the
  mixer says so.
- **Background music**: on or off for this narration, its volume, and the track.

## Turn music on

1. Start reading a document.
2. Open the **Audio mixer**.
3. Turn on **Background music**.

The music fades in. It plays while the narration plays, fades out when you pause, and carries on from
the same place when you press play again. Moving to another sentence or section, changing the voice,
the speed or the pace, and a sleep timer don't interrupt it.

**Music is off again for every new narration.** Opening a different document, reaching the end of the
document, or closing and reopening TTSNative turns it off. Turn it on again whenever you want it.
TTSNative remembers which track you chose and how loud you like it, but never turns it on by itself.

## Choose a track

Select **Browse** in the audio mixer to open the library:

- **Categories** (Ambient, Fantasy, Cinematic, Lo-fi, Focus, Nature) narrow the list.
- **Preview** (the play button beside a track) lets you hear a track for up to 20 seconds. If music is
  playing, the preview replaces it for a moment; the narration keeps going. Closing the library stops
  the preview. Previewing never turns music on.
- **Select a track** to use it. If music is playing, it crossfades to the new track.

## Volume

The music's volume goes from 0 to 100 %. It starts at 10 %, which keeps the music well below the
voice. It is separate from the narration: the music slider never changes how loud the narration is,
and the narration slider never changes the music. Both are saved on this device; another computer or
phone keeps its own.

loud parts. They run from about a minute and a half to eight minutes, and each one loops smoothly,
so it can play for as long as you listen. However long a track is, playing it takes the same small
amount of memory: TTSNative plays it in pieces of about 20 seconds, blended into each other.

was edited.

## Privacy

The music ships with TTSNative and plays from your own computer. Nothing is downloaded or sent anywhere,
and it works without an internet connection.

## Exported audio

Exports contain the narration only. Music isn't added to exported files.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Couldn't play …"**: that track couldn't be loaded. The narration carries on; choose another
  track or turn music on again.
- **"Background music isn't available in this browser."**: the browser can't play music this way.
  Narration still works. Use a current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
- **The music is too loud or too quiet**: adjust the music's volume in the audio mixer, not the
  narration's.
