# Keyboard shortcuts

These shortcuts work on any page once a document is loaded in the player.

| Keys                 | Action                                                |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| `Space`, `K`         | Play or pause                                         |
| `←`, `→`             | Previous or next sentence                             |
| `Shift ←`, `Shift →` | Previous or next section                              |
| `[`, `]`             | Slower or faster                                      |
| `B`                  | Bookmark the sentence being read (again to remove it) |
| `?`                  | Show keyboard shortcuts                               |

In each row, the first key does the first action and the second key the second (for example, `←` goes
to the previous sentence and `→` to the next).

## Good to know

- **Speed steps.** `[` and `]` move through the playback speeds 0.75×, 0.9×, 1×, 1.1×, 1.25×, 1.5×,
  1.75× and 2×. The new speed is saved as your default.
- **When shortcuts are ignored.** They don't fire while you're typing in a field, inside a dialog, or
  while a button, link, slider or other control has focus, so `Space` still presses the focused
  button. They're also ignored while `Ctrl`, `Alt` or `Cmd` is held.
- **The shortcut list.** Press `?` or, on wide screens, the keyboard button in the player bar.
- **Media controls.** Where your browser supports it, the lock screen and other system media controls
  can play and pause, and their previous and next track buttons move one sentence back or forward.

Sections are the chapters, headings or pages a document is divided into; see
[Reading Documents](Reading-Documents#chapters-sections-and-pages).
